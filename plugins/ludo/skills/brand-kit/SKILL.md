# Brand kit

A brand for a student project doesn't need an agency. It needs a clear idea, one strong mark, two fonts, a few colors, and the discipline to use them the same way everywhere.

## Step 1, the idea behind it

Ask, multiple choice where possible:
- What is it, who is it for, and what should people feel? (trusted, bold, playful, exclusive, calm)
- Three words it should be, and one word it must never be.
- Any existing colors, school ties or names to respect?

Write a one-sentence brand idea, like "Serious tools, student prices" or "The quiet club that builds loud things".

## Step 2, name check (if naming)

For a new name: check it's pronounceable, not already a well-known brand in the same space (search), and whether the .com or handle is free (search; say it's unconfirmed if you can't tell).

## Step 3, logo concepts as SVG

Draw three different directions in `logo-concepts.html`, each as inline SVG:
1. **Wordmark**: the name in a well-chosen typeface, with one small custom detail.
2. **Symbol**: a simple geometric mark with a meaning tied to the brand idea.
3. **Monogram**: initials in a compact badge.

Rules: works in one color, readable at 32px, no gradients, no clip art, no trendy effects. Show each on light and dark backgrounds and at small size. Explain the meaning of each in one line.

## Step 4, the system

- **Palette**: one main color, one accent, two neutrals, with hex codes and contrast checked (text must pass WCAG AA). See the palette table in `/ludo:design`.
- **Type**: a heading font and a body font, both free on Google Fonts.
- **Voice**: three do's and three don'ts for how the brand writes.

## Step 5, the brand sheet

One page, `brand-sheet.html` (prints to PDF): logo in its versions, palette swatches with hex codes, type specimens, voice rules, and two examples in use (an Instagram post and an email header). Export the chosen logo as SVG and PNG (512px and 1024px).

## Rules

Original work only: never copy or closely imitate another organization's logo, name or trade dress. For image-generation tools, only use ones the user has access to and rights for.
