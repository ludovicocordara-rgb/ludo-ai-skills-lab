# Citation quick reference

Examples use a made-up journal article and web page. Always check edge cases against the official guide.

## APA 7
Journal: Rivera, J., & Chen, M. (2024). Title of the article in sentence case. *Journal Name in Title Case*, *12*(3), 45–67. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx
Web page: Rivera, J. (2025, March 4). *Title of the page in sentence case*. Site Name. https://example.com/page
In text: (Rivera & Chen, 2024, p. 50). Three or more authors: (Rivera et al., 2024).
No date: (Rivera, n.d.). List: alphabetical by first author, hanging indent, double spaced.

## MLA 9
Journal: Rivera, Jordan, and Maya Chen. "Title of the Article in Title Case." *Journal Name*, vol. 12, no. 3, 2024, pp. 45–67, https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.
Web page: Rivera, Jordan. "Title of the Page." *Site Name*, 4 Mar. 2025, example.com/page.
In text: (Rivera and Chen 50). Three or more authors: (Rivera et al. 50).
List: "Works Cited", alphabetical, hanging indent, double spaced.

## Chicago 17, notes and bibliography
Note: 1. Jordan Rivera and Maya Chen, "Title of the Article," *Journal Name* 12, no. 3 (2024): 50, https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.
Short note: 2. Rivera and Chen, "Title of the Article," 52.
Bibliography: Rivera, Jordan, and Maya Chen. "Title of the Article." *Journal Name* 12, no. 3 (2024): 45–67. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.

## Where to get the real details
- DOI metadata: https://api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>
- Books: https://openlibrary.org/search?q=<title>
- Never guess an author, date or page. Leave it out the way the style says to.
