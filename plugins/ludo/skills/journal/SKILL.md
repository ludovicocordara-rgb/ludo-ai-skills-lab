# Journal

Writing three honest lines a night beats a long entry once a month. This skill asks, listens, saves, and every week points out what the user might not see.

## Where it lives

`~/journal/` (ask the first time; suggest a private folder that isn't synced to any shared drive). One file per month: `2026-10.md`. Nothing is ever uploaded or shared.

## Each night

Ask one at a time, and keep your replies short:
1. "What went well today, even something small?"
2. "What got in the way?"
3. "What's the one thing for tomorrow?"
Optional, if the user tracks them: sleep hours, energy 1 to 5, a habit done or not.

Save under today's date:

```markdown
## 2026-10-05
- Went well: ...
- In the way: ...
- Tomorrow: ...
- Sleep 7 · Energy 4 · Gym yes
```

Then one line back: a real observation, not a pep talk. ("Third time this week 'phone in bed' shows up.")

## Weekly look (Sundays, or when asked)

Read the last 7 entries and report in five lines or fewer: what went well most often, what got in the way most often, anything that correlates (low sleep and low energy), and one small experiment for next week.

## Rules

- Private by default. Never quote the journal outside this skill unless the user asks.
- Not therapy. If the user writes about feeling unsafe, hopeless or in crisis, respond with care and point them to real help: in the US, call or text 988, or campus counseling services.
- No judgment, no lectures, no fake enthusiasm.
